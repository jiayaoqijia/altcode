A `select` statement blocks until one of its `case` channel operations can proceed, then executes that case — if multiple are ready simultaneously, one is chosen at random. A `default` case makes it non-blocking, executing immediately if no channel is ready. It's the idiomatic way to multiplex, timeout (`time.After`), or cancel (`ctx.Done()`) across multiple concurrent channel operations.
